Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard ☕📊

An interactive, professionally designed Excel dashboard built to analyze and visualize comprehensive coffee shop sales data, customer footfall, and product performance metrics.

📋 Overview

This project provides a visually appealing and data-rich management dashboard tailored for a coffee shop business (or multi-location franchise). It aggregates key performance indicators (KPIs), hourly trends, category breakdowns, product sizes, store-level comparisons, and day-wise revenue streams into a unified, user-friendly interface.

🚀 Key Features & Visualizations

1. High-Level KPI Cards

Total Sales: Tracks overall revenue ($6,98,812.33).

Total Footfall: Measures total customer traffic/visits (149,116).

AOV (Average Order Value): Displays the average transaction value ($4.69).

2. Interactive Slicers

Day Name Slicer: Filter the entire dashboard dynamically by specific days of the week (Sunday through Saturday).

Month Name Slicer: Filter data seamlessly across operational months (January through June).

3. Analytical Charts & Graphs

Quantities Based On Hours (Line Chart): Illustrates hourly transaction and product quantity trends from 6 AM to 8 PM, highlighting peak operational hours (notably around 9 AM – 11 AM).

Distribution Based on Sales (Pie Chart): Breaks down revenue contributions across product categories such as Coffee, Bakery, Branded merchandise, Coffee beans, Drinking Chocolate, and Flavours.

Quantities Sold Based on Size (Pie Chart): Analyzes sales distribution by product sizes (Large, Regular, Small, and Not Defined).

Footfall and Sales of Store (Bar/Column Comparison): Compares customer footfall and total sales across multiple store locations (Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, Lower Manhattan).

Top 5 Products Based on Sales (Bar Chart): Highlights top-performing menu items like Barista Espresso, Brewed Black Tea, Brewed Chai Tea, Gourmet Brewed Coffee, and Hot Chocolate.

Day Wise Sales (Column Chart): Details revenue fluctuations across every day of the week to identify busy and slow operating days.

🛠️ Technology & Tools Used

Microsoft Excel: Built entirely using advanced Excel features including Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Formulas, and interactive Slicers.

Design Theme: Warm, cohesive coffee-themed aesthetic (warm beige/orange tones with dark brown accents for readability and professional presentation).

💡 How to Use the Dashboard

Open the Excel workbook (.xlsx).

Navigate to the Dashboard tab.

Use the Day Name and Month Name slicers on the left-hand panel to slice and dice the data dynamically.

Review the KPI cards and visual charts to gain instant business insights regarding peak hours, top products, and store performance.
