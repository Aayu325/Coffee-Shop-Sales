# ☕📊 Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard

An interactive, professionally designed Excel dashboard built to analyze and visualize comprehensive coffee shop sales data, customer footfall, and product performance metrics.

---

## 📋 Overview

This project provides a visually appealing and data-rich management dashboard tailored for a coffee shop business (or multi-location franchise). It aggregates key performance indicators (KPIs), hourly trends, category breakdowns, product sizes, store-level comparisons, and day-wise revenue streams into a unified, user-friendly interface.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Visualizations

### 1. High-Level KPI Cards
- **Total Sales:** Tracks overall revenue ($6,98,812.33)
- **Total Footfall:** Measures total customer traffic/visits (149,116)
- **AOV (Average Order Value):** Displays the average transaction value ($4.69)

---

### 2. Interactive Slicers
- **Day Name Slicer:** Filter the entire dashboard dynamically by specific days of the week (Sunday–Saturday)
- **Month Name Slicer:** Filter data seamlessly across operational months (January–June)

---

### 3. Analytical Charts & Graphs

#### 📈 Quantities Based on Hours (Line Chart)
Illustrates hourly transaction and product quantity trends from 6 AM to 8 PM, highlighting peak operational hours (notably 9 AM – 11 AM).

#### 🥧 Distribution Based on Sales (Pie Chart)
Breaks down revenue contributions across product categories:
- Coffee  
- Bakery  
- Branded merchandise  
- Coffee beans  
- Drinking Chocolate  
- Flavours  

#### 🥧 Quantities Sold Based on Size (Pie Chart)
Analyzes sales distribution by product sizes:
- Large  
- Regular  
- Small  
- Not Defined  

#### 📊 Footfall and Sales of Store (Bar/Column Comparison)
Compares customer footfall and total sales across multiple store locations:
- Astoria  
- Hell's Kitchen  
- Lower Manhattan  

#### 📊 Top 5 Products Based on Sales (Bar Chart)
Highlights top-performing menu items:
- Barista Espresso  
- Brewed Black Tea  
- Brewed Chai Tea  
- Gourmet Brewed Coffee  
- Hot Chocolate  

#### 📊 Day Wise Sales (Column Chart)
Details revenue fluctuations across every day of the week to identify busy and slow operating days.

## 💡 How to Use the Dashboard

1. Open the Excel workbook (`.xlsx`)
2. Navigate to the **Dashboard** tab
3. Use the **Day Name** and **Month Name** slicers to filter data dynamically
4. Explore KPI cards and charts for insights on:
   - Peak hours
   - Top products
   - Store performance
   - Sales trends
